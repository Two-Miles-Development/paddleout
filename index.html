Watch with subtitles in your language

Some videos are in English and some are in Japanese. Either way, YouTube can turn the speech into subtitles and translate them into your language automatically.

On a computer

  1. Play the video on YouTube and click the CC (subtitles) button at the bottom right of the player.
  2. Click the gear icon (Settings), then Subtitles/CC.
  3. 🌐Choose Auto-translate, then pick your language.

On a smartphone (YouTube app)

  1. ▶Open the video in the YouTube app and tap the video once to show the controls.
  2. Tap the gear icon (or ⋮) at the top, then Captions.
  3. 🌐Choose Auto-translate, then pick your language.

The subtitles are generated and translated automatically by YouTube, so some wording may differ slightly from what is actually said. The menu names may also vary a little by device and app version.
